## Categories

### [Crawlability and Indexing](categories/crawlability.md)
12 checks covering index coverage, robots.txt, redirects, broken links, sitemaps, crawl budget, IndexNow, and orphan pages.

### [Core Web Vitals and Performance](categories/core-web-vitals.md)
8 checks for LCP, INP, CLS, minification, caching, CDN, image optimization, and TTFB.

### [Site Architecture](categories/site-architecture.md)
7 checks for URL structure, breadcrumbs, internal linking, navigation, silos, and orphan pages.

### [Structured Data and Schema](categories/schema-markup.md)
6 checks for Article, Organization, Breadcrumb, FAQ, Product schema, and Rich Results testing.

### [Security and HTTPS](categories/security.md)
4 checks for SSL, mixed content, HSTS, and security headers.

### [Mobile and UX](categories/mobile-ux.md)
5 checks for mobile-friendliness, viewport, interstitials, touch targets, and readability.

### [AI Search and GEO](categories/ai-search-geo.md)
6 checks for AI crawler access, semantic HTML, entity optimization, llms.txt, and agentic commerce.

### [Technical SEO Audit](categories/audit-checklist.md)
16 checks organized by frequency: weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual.

### [For Non-Technical Marketers](categories/for-beginners.md)
5 checks for non-developers: what you can do yourself, when to hire help, and free tools.
